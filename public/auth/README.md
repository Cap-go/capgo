`google-sign-in.png` is the unmodified Android + Web, Light, icon-only,
Square, 4x asset from Google's official sign-in branding bundle:
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip

The button displays the centered Google G from this asset at its original
20px design size. Button styling follows:
https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines

Google Sans Medium is self-hosted under `public/fonts/google-sans/` with
its SIL Open Font License. No external font request is made at runtime.
